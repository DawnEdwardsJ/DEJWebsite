How we work together

From policy to practice.

1. Advisory

I work with management to shape a menopause policy that fits your people and your organisation — practical, compassionate and clear.

2. Implementation

We put the policy into practice — manager training, staff education and the everyday support that makes it real.

3. Embedding

Ongoing guidance so the policy becomes part of how your workplace actually operates.

Leadership team in a considered planning session
Why it matters

A policy is only as good as its practice.

Too many organisations write a menopause policy and stop there. My role is to make sure it lives — shaping the document with your leadership, then guiding the training, communication and everyday support that turn intention into culture.

  • Advisory partnership with management
  • Policy shaped to your organisation
  • Manager and staff education
  • Practical implementation and follow-through
